02 / Our philosophy

Beauty in the space between.

Japanese ceramics invite us to notice what is often overlooked: the weight of a cup, the quiet line of a rim, the subtle movement of glaze. KASANE is a study in useful objects and lasting presence.

03 / Care & details

Made to be lived with.

A few notes on care, variation, and the journey from our collection to your home.

Is it dishwasher and microwave safe?

Care depends on the final piece and glaze. For the sample collection, gentle hand washing is recommended to preserve each surface.

Why does my piece look a little different from the photo?

Every piece is thrown and glazed by hand, and glazes move in the kiln. Expect small variations in size, speckling, and color. That's what makes yours one of a kind.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping timing will be confirmed when the collection launches. Each piece will be packed with care for its journey.

What if something arrives broken?

Please contact the studio with a photograph and your order details so we can make it right.

Do you take custom orders?

Please contact the studio to discuss a considered commission or special order.
